# Social & Events — Category Rules

> Canonical Social scope: Events, Meetups and Community Activities.

## 1. Allowed Social types

```text
event
meetup
community_activity
```

No partner-oriented Social subtype is allowed.

## 2. Human Connections boundary

Move to Human Connections when the main intent is:

> find a person/group for me.

Keep in Social & Events when the main intent is:

> publish an organized event/meetup/activity.

Do not automatically duplicate the same Listing across both categories.

## 3. Mandatory moderation

Use the normal Advertio publication flow:

```text
Submit
→ Pending Review
→ Admin Approve / Reject
→ Active
```

## 4. Contact monetization

Social & Events contact is free.

- No Early Access Coin required by default.
- Use the official CTA for analytics.
- External registration can be supported.
- Event admission price is not an Advertio contact fee.

## 5. Multiple active Listings

Organizers may legitimately run multiple events/meetups.

Social & Events therefore allows multiple Active Listings subject to:

- moderation;
- spam/rate controls;
- duplicate checks.

Do not enforce a global one-active-listing-per-category database constraint here.

## 6. Date/lifecycle

Every Active Social Listing must have a usable current/future schedule.

One-time Listings should leave upcoming discovery after their end/start window according to lifecycle rules.

Recurring Listings must keep an explicit recurrence definition.

## 7. Location and online safety

For in-person activities:

- store canonical public discovery location;
- avoid requiring sensitive private home-address information as a filter field.

For online activities:

- do not expose private meeting credentials unnecessarily in public structured attributes.

## 8. Registration URLs

External URLs:

- HTTPS only;
- subject to moderation/security checks;
- clearly indicate that the user is leaving Advertio.

## 9. Admission

Admission values may be informational:

- free;
- paid;
- donation;
- unspecified.

Advertio V1 does not promise:

- ticket settlement;
- event refund;
- attendance guarantee;
- escrow.

## 10. Trust

Organizer verification badges must correspond to real platform verification states.

Do not infer organizer trust from event popularity, attendance claims or description text.

## 11. Partner files removed

These old Social concepts are no longer valid Social files:

- sports-partner
- study-partner
- other-partner

Their person-seeking use cases belong to Human Connections.

## 12. Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Only Event/Meetup/Community Activity types are accepted.
- [ ] Partner requests route to Human Connections.
- [ ] Contact is free.
- [ ] Multiple legitimate Social Listings are supported.
- [ ] Schedule controls active/upcoming discovery.
- [ ] External registration/admission does not imply ticketing support.
