## CAC definition

Source:
- CAC = مجموع هزینه جذب، ثبت‌نام و تبدیل User به Active + Paying state.

Full CAC formula در Source:

```text
Full CAC
= actual member acquisition cost
+ operational costs
+ adjustment based on conversion rate
```

اما Full CAC واقعی نهایی محاسبه نشده است.

## General Jobs market sizing — Canada

Status: Source estimate / market analysis

### General-job share
- Job Bank: 12,578 General از 63,119 = 19.9%
- Kijiji: 11,321 General از 68,292 = 16.6%
- Average General share used by Source: ~18%

### Audience calculation
- Telegram users in Canada: ~3,000,000
- Job-seeker ratio: 3.6%
- 3,000,000 × 3.6% = ~108,000 job seekers
- 108,000 × 18% = ~19,400 General Jobs users
