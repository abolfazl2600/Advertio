## 1. Who we are and scope

Advertio is a classifieds and user-connection platform for housing/rentals and roommates, passenger-cargo matching, and job opportunities. Users may browse listings, create accounts, submit listings, communicate with other users, report content, use Telegram-based services and, where offered, purchase digital Advertio Coins for platform functions.

This policy describes how **[LEGAL OPERATOR]** collects, uses, discloses, retains and protects personal information in the Advertio website, web application, Telegram Mini App/bot, support processes and Advertio-controlled distribution channels. Other parties' services, properties, employment offers or shipments are not operated by Advertio merely because they are listed here.

## 2. Personal information we handle

Depending on how you use Advertio and the functions enabled, we may process:

- **Account and contact information:** name/display name, phone number and phone-verification status, Telegram identifiers/username, avatar, language preferences, authentication identifiers and relevant account settings.
- **Listings and media:** the information, descriptions, photos and location/route attributes you submit; publication status, moderation decisions, listing history, category and relevant contact details. A submitted listing may be visible to visitors, users and connected distribution channels.
- **Messages and support:** in-app conversations, reports, feedback, correspondence with support, user requests and their resolution.
- **Verification information:** where you choose to request paid identity verification, identification document image, challenge video, verification result and related audit records. Phone verification and identity-document verification are separate; identity verification is not confirmation of title to a property or of the safety of a person, cargo or transaction.
- **Payments and Coins:** coin balance, service charges, purchases, payment status, transaction and ledger references, refund status and, for enabled manual-payment methods, submitted payment notes or receipt images. Card payment data is processed by the applicable payment provider; **[CONFIRM WHETHER ANY CARD DATA IS RECEIVED OR STORED BY ADVERTIO]**.
- **Usage and technical records:** language or interface preferences, session/security identifiers, access timestamps, device/browser information, security logs and activity needed to deliver or protect the service; specific analytics providers and tracking technologies must be itemized in **[DATA INVENTORY / COOKIE DISCLOSURE]**.
- **Third-party-source listings:** information contained in listings imported from Telegram or other sources **only where Advertio has a valid basis and permission to collect, display and use it**. This may include listing text, images, contact handle/number, source URL and time/source metadata. A person appearing in an imported listing may not have created an Advertio account.

We should not ask you to submit sensitive ID documents, financial details or unrelated personal information inside a public listing or ordinary chat.

## 3. Why we use information

We use personal information, as applicable, to:
1. create, authenticate, maintain and secure accounts, including phone verification;
2. receive, display, search, moderate, distribute, renew and remove listings;
3. facilitate user-to-user contact and messaging and manage reports and abuse investigations;
4. process in-platform purchases and service access, maintain auditable transactions and handle refunds;
5. conduct optional identity-document verification upon a user-initiated request;
6. send requested transactional messages and saved-search alerts, and commercial messages **only where a valid legal basis/consent or exception applies**;
7. provide customer support, administer rights requests, comply with law and protect users and the service;
8. maintain technical logs and improve performance in ways consistent with applicable law and disclosed settings.

We will identify material new purposes and obtain further consent where required. We will not treat an unpublished, third-party listing as proof that its owner agreed to Advertio marketing.

## 5. Public listings, contact access and Telegram distribution

Information intentionally included in a public listing can be seen or copied by others. Depending on the listing, contact details may be masked until the viewer completes Advertio's phone verification or coin-based unlock. Do not publish information you do not have permission to share.

Advertio can distribute listings through Advertio-controlled Telegram channels and related tools. Once information is transmitted to a third-party platform or downloaded by others, removal from Advertio may not eliminate every external copy. We will handle requests involving copies within our control and explain practical limitations.

Where a listing has been imported from an external source, Advertio should identify it as third-party-sourced and provide a means for the affected person to request removal or correction. **[IMPLEMENT AND VERIFY IMPORTED-LISTING NOTICE / REMOVAL WORKFLOW BEFORE APPROVAL.]**
