### Research quality rules

1. Distinguish marketing claims from tested product behavior.
2. Capture the date for every dynamic metric and price.
3. Test both sender and traveler flows.
4. Compare identical routes, directions, item types, and weights.
5. Never treat members, downloads, listings, or registrations as completed transactions.
6. Record contradictions rather than silently choosing the most favorable claim.
7. Do not recommend copying a feature until its user value and operating cost are understood.
