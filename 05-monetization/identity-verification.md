# Paid Identity Verification

> Product Decision — 24 Sep 2026  
> This document records a new approved Advertio product/monetization decision. It is not derived from the original Source Document.

## Purpose

Advertio will offer a paid **Identity Verification** service.

A successful verification grants the user the explicit trust badge:

**Identity Verified**

This badge is separate from **Phone verified** and any other verification layer.

## Verification model

The initial version is manually reviewed through Backoffice.

The user submits:

1. a supported government-issued identity document; and
2. a video completing a randomized challenge generated by Advertio.

The randomized challenge is intended to make static prerecorded submissions less useful and to give the admin a concrete instruction to verify against the submitted video.

The exact supported document types and challenge catalogue may evolve.

## Initial price

Initial service price:

**USD $8 equivalent**

Supported payment paths:

- Advertio Coins;
- other payment methods supported by Advertio.

The current documented Coin reference is **10 Coin = $1**, which implies an initial reference price of **80 Coin**.

The canonical pricing/payment configuration must remain the source of truth instead of hardcoding the amount separately across clients.

## Monetization role

Identity Verification is a direct paid trust service.

Revenue is generated when a user chooses to pay for a stronger trust signal and completes the verification workflow.

The fee is charged for the verification service/review process; payment alone does not grant the badge.

## Backoffice operation

Backoffice must support a review queue containing:

- request ID;
- user;
- payment status/method/reference;
- identity document;
- assigned randomized video challenge;
- submitted video;
- verification status;
- reviewing admin;
- review timestamp;
- approval/rejection result.

Only successful admin approval grants **Identity Verified**.

## User-visible states

Suggested lifecycle:

- Awaiting payment
- Awaiting documents
- Submitted
- Under review
- Approved
- Rejected

Payment status and verification status are separate.

## Security

Identity documents and verification videos are sensitive data.

They must:
- be visible only to authorized roles;
- use secure storage/media access;
- not be published through public URLs;
- not be copied into ordinary analytics/log payloads;
- remain auditable through safe references and admin-action logs.
